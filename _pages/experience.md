**Biostatistician/RWE Analyst,** Michigan State University, *09/2022-Present*

**The Long-term Causal Effect of Pesticide Exposure on Olfactory Impairment (OI) among U.S. Farmers (Ph.D. Dissertation, 2024-Present)** 

- Leveraging data from the U.S. Agricultural Health Study (AHS)—an ongoing long-term prospective cohort of over 52,000 licensed farmers followed across multiple phases over 30 years with comprehensive longitudinal pesticide exposure assessments—and its sub-cohort, the Pesticide and the Sense of Smell (PASS) study, a stratified subsample of Phase 4 participants selected using predefined sampling weights.
- Co-designed the study in collaboration with faculty mentors, contributing to exposure and outcome definition and considerations around sampling strategies for the PASS sub-study.
- Performed data cleaning, harmonization, and quality control for complex epidemiologic datasets, preparing high-quality longitudinal data for downstream statistical analysis.
- Applying causal inference methods using a stochastic intervention framework incorporating missing-data mechanisms, accounting for continuous exposures, complex sampling, and nonresponse bias.

**High Pesticide Exposure Events and Neurologic Outcomes among U.S. Farmers (2022-Present)**

- Investigated the association between high pesticide exposure events (HPEEs) and neurologic outcomes—including olfactory impairment (OI) and subjective cognitive complaints—among older farmers using large-scale longitudinal survey data from the AHS.
- Co-designed the study in collaboration with faculty mentors, conducted data cleaning, harmonization, and management for complex longitudinal datasets.
- Applied TMLE with inverse probability of missingness weighting to estimate marginal exposure–outcome contrasts while accounting for sampling design, nonresponse, and high-dimensional covariates.
- Implemented supervised learning with 10-fold cross-validation, combining baseline, regularized, and advanced machine learning models to capture complex exposure-response patterns while minimizing overfitting.
- Led manuscript preparation as first author for the OI analysis (under review) and contributed as third author to the cognitive complaints analysis (published).
- Project supported by the National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences (NIEHS), and in part by the Intramural Research Programs of National Institutes of Health (NIH), NIEHS, and the National Cancer Institute (NCI).

**Economic conditions during pregnancy and preterm birth/birth weight (2022-2023)**

- Leveraged data from the Health, Aging, and Body Composition (Health ABC) study, a long-term prospective cohort of 3,075 older adults (1997–1998), with up to 17 years of follow-up through clinical visits, phone assessments, and hospitalization/death surveillance.
- Co-designed the study in collaboration with faculty mentors, conducted data cleaning, harmonization, and management for complex epidemiologic datasets.

- Co-designed the study in collaboration with faculty mentors, conducted data cleaning, harmonization, and management for complex epidemiologic datasets.
